ReaLiDAR: Material-Aware LiDAR Simulation for Robust 3D Sensing in Complex Environments

LiDAR has become a ubiquitous 3D sensing modality for autonomous driving, robotics, and unmanned aerial vehicles, where simulation promises scalable, labeled data at near-zero annotation cost. Existing simulators, however, rely on single-bounce ray casting or data-driven point-cloud modeling and miss material-dependent scattering, leaving a persistent sim-to-real gap. ReaLiDAR closes this gap with a material-aware framework built on radiance-based light transport: it represents each surface with a parameterized material model and traces laser pulses through multi-bounce interactions, yielding high-fidelity point clouds for both indoor and outdoor scenes.

ReaLiDAR consists of 4 modules: Laser Beam Modeling, Light Transport Modeling, Material Reflectance Modeling, and Post-Processing.
